Cue Captain privacy

No app account is required to author, play, or share routines.

Local routines and sharing

Saved routines, settings, and imported sounds are stored on your device, or in browser storage for the web app. You choose when to export or share them through other services.

Self-contained links and QR codes carry authored snapshots in a URL fragment. Anyone with the complete link can read it. Links exclude custom sounds and Library metadata; file archives can carry ready custom sounds. Each explicit link import creates an independent local copy, with no account, revocation, or update service.

The isolated browser preview decodes on your device. It makes no routine-payload requests, saves no routine, uses no storage or telemetry, and does not download sounds. Installation links contain no routine fragment. After installation, reopen the original routine link.

External hosting and voluntary support

The public site uses GitHub Pages and the web app uses Cloudflare Pages. Hosts receive ordinary requests and network metadata such as IP addresses; URL fragments are not sent in HTTP requests. Link values may still be retained by services or people you choose to share them with. Neither public page adds tracking scripts.

The Google feedback form and email are optional external support channels. The developer receives only information you choose to submit; these services process submissions under their own policies. Do not submit sensitive information or private routine content. Support does not attach routines, sounds, or diagnostics automatically.

Existing opt-in diagnostics

Diagnostics are off by default and controlled in Settings per device. When configured, explicit opt-in enables sanitized Sentry crash reports and coarse Aptabase usage events on iOS/Android. Web has no Aptabase usage events. Opt-out stops providers; it does not delete previously received records. App-authored diagnostic events exclude routine contents, link payloads, custom sounds, free text, voice IDs, contacts, health data, and authored location.

Diagnostic services receive technical app, device/session, and connection metadata. The configured services use US hosting. Aptabase derives and stores country/region and a daily pseudonymous identifier from connection metadata; it processes IP addresses and User-Agent transiently and documents event retention of up to five years. Sentry may retain IP addresses, device/installation metadata, and IP-derived approximate location (such as city, country, and region) with diagnostics. Its event retention follows the applicable plan and data type; the current Developer plan documents 30-day event retention. Provider backups can follow a separate expiry schedule.

Cue Captain does not require contacts, location, camera, microphone, wearable, Apple Health, or Health Connect access.

Privacy requests and retention

To request deletion of a support submission or identifiable diagnostic record, email andremunay@gmail.com with the subject Cue Captain privacy request. Include an approximate submission/incident date and the minimum information needed to locate it. We may ask for a reply from the original support address to verify ownership. Do not send passwords, verification codes, identity documents, routine archives, or routine-link fragments.

We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 calendar days and resolve them or provide a specific progress response within 30 calendar days. Applicable shorter deadlines take priority. Ordinary support emails and form submissions are deleted 90 days after case closure. A minimal request/outcome log is retained for 12 months after closure, without copied routine contents, attachments, or diagnostic payloads. Necessary legal or security exceptions are documented with restricted access and a review date.

We explain what was deleted, any necessary retained data, and provider or backup limits. Some diagnostic data cannot be associated with a person from an email address alone; Aptabase may be unable to identify an individual's analytics without additional information. We do not add tracking to identify otherwise anonymous records or promise deletion of records we cannot identify or control. Local routine/sound deletion uses the app's device controls and is separate from a provider deletion request.
